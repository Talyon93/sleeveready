# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Players of trading card games who want to try cards before buying them. The home serves first those who play a newer game (Disney Lorcana, Star Wars Unlimited, Riftbound, Cyberpunk TCG, One Piece): they come for spoilers and freshly released sets and print them to playtest. Other visitors are players who arrive with a decklist ready (Moxfield, Archidekt, plain text) and players who land from search on a single card or illustrator page. The audience is worldwide, the USA is the main market; about half the visits are on desktop, since printing happens there.

## Product Purpose
SleeveReady (sleeveready.com) turns cards into print sheets for playtest proxies: pick an expansion or paste a list, check the sheet, print at 100% on A4 or US Letter (3 × 3 cards at 63 × 88 mm, other sizes per game). Card images and data come from each game's official sources, in every language the card was printed in. Success is a visitor who leaves with a printed sheet and comes back for the next set.

## Positioning
Sixteen card games in one place, each with its official card list, upcoming sets and spoilers as soon as they are revealed. Free, with no account needed to print; the list stays on the device.

## Capabilities
- Catalogue per game and expansion, upcoming sets with revealed cards, card and illustrator pages, most-printed rankings.
- Decklist import (plain text, Archidekt links; Moxfield when it grants access), shareable lists, saved lists with an account (Google or magic link).
- Print options: paper sizes, bleed up to 3 mm, crop marks, card backs for duplex, Cricut Print Then Cut PNGs, Silhouette marks, SVG cut files, PDF download.
- Interface in 8 languages, English by default.
- Shop links to the original cards and sleeves (Amazon, eBay, TCGplayer, CardTrader) as affiliate income.

## Constraints
- Proxies are for playtesting only: never present them as substitutes or counterfeits of real cards.
- Terminology: "list", not "deck".
- No third-party analytics or cookies (so no consent banner); own anonymous counters only.
- Hosting cost matters (Netlify credits): prefer static pages; avoid work per crawler request.

## Voice
Plain and direct, no slogans or promotional badges. The user asked for the site to feel smooth and cool, never like generic AI-made design.
